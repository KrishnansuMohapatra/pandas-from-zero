# 🐼 Final Project — E-Commerce Sales Analysis

This is my final Pandas project from the Pandas-from-Zero series.

The goal is to take an e-commerce sales dataset and turn it into useful business information.

## What I practiced

- Data inspection
- Date conversion
- Aggregation
- GroupBy
- Product analysis
- City analysis
- Category analysis
- Monthly analysis
- Finding best and lowest performers
- Writing simple business insights

## Questions I answered

- What is the total revenue?
- What is the average sale?
- Which product generated the most revenue?
- Which city performed best?
- Which category generated the most revenue?
- Which month had the highest sales?
- What recommendations can be made from the data?

## My findings

- Bhubaneswar performed the best with ₹313,000 in revenue.
- Laptop generated the most product revenue with ₹300,000.
- Mouse sold the most units, but its lower price meant lower total revenue.
- Electronics generated ₹640,000 compared with ₹84,000 from Accessories.
- April had the highest monthly sales at ₹176,500.
- Bhubaneswar and Cuttack were the strongest markets, so their performance is worth investigating before making further marketing decisions.

This project helped me understand that data analysis is not only about getting numbers from Pandas. The important part is understanding what those numbers mean.
